# First three settlements: five-edition coverage queue

User-confirmed scope: **Pallet, Viridian, Pewter** in FireRed/LeafGreen and
**Littleroot, Oldale, Petalburg** in Emerald/Ruby/Sapphire. Include connecting
routes, Viridian Forest, route buildings and every interior in these regions.
Route 103 is also included as an introductory Hoenn route. Route 22 and the
journey beyond Petalburg are outside the connecting-route minimum.

A map stays queued until native source, rendered views, walking/entry clearance
and its distinct edition art have been reviewed. Existing classifiers/models
are a starting point, not proof of complete visual coverage.

The requested institutional batch is separate; see
[its evidence](GEN3_RS_INSTITUTIONS_2026-10-15.md).

Service-room batch 1.43.0 verifies 30 of the 125 edition-map placements below.
Home batch 1.45.0 adds 27 Hoenn placements. Kanto home/school batch 1.46.0
adds 14 placements. Laboratory batch1.47.0 adds five placements
(76 verified total);49 remain queued.
See [the service-room evidence](GEN3_SERVICE_ROOMS_2026-10-15.md).
See [the home evidence](GEN3_HOMES_2026-10-17.md) and
[Kanto home/school evidence](GEN3_KANTO_HOMES_2026-10-17.md), plus
[laboratory evidence](GEN3_LABS_2026-10-17.md).
A source/render capture alone does not complete a queued map.

Walls must be flat and continuous, with no gaps or overlapping grey backing.
Furniture is separate in front. Stairs follow original art and walking cells.
Characters, Pokémon and flowers remain HD-2D; partners remain optional.
