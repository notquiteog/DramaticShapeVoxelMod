# R/S/E native-art piers: closed boards and open gaps

Source-only implementation; native GPU review remains pending. This does not
claim an imported ORAS dock model. Uses the user's live native source atlas;
no ROM drawing, extracted tile PNG, or cache is bundled.

The audited imported R/S/E layouts agree exactly:

| Dewford, x11–13/y9–10 | general__dewford | 228/229/229;230/231/231 | 0/0/0;7/7/7 | 3/3/3;0/0/0 |

The blue inter-board pixels are opaque in the native under atlas. Alpha blending
alone cannot open them. Unrecognized piers previously received a whole tile
plane plus ORAS earth from ShoreBanks/BankMaterials. Merely adding the old bridge
fascia would retain a whole-tile underside, also sealing every gap from below.

Gen3Docks validates the complete map/pair/MID/permission/layer/level assembly,
checks conflicting geometry ownership, then marks its cells as `bridge.kind =
'dock'`. Native data is unchanged, including blocked and surf fringe cells.
Each six-pixel board has its own top, bottom, ends and sides, with two-pixel
spaces between neighboring boards. Timber is two pixels thick. Source grain
and nails remain on top and timber-only source UV strips supply upright sides.
All boards share the real deck height even when their source drawing occupies
a surf fringe with a lower native floor. The source's bottom-row projected
shadow becomes actual open space below the boards. Dewford keeps native sand
patches at the landward transition. No whole-tile deck underside is emitted.

## Scene hook contract

The generic Bridges.prepare pass is too early for guarded piers: native floor
fields and region ownership have not yet been assigned. After Elevation.bind
and `c.region` assignment, immediately before NativeWater.bind, call
`Bridges.prepareDocks(cells)` and add its count to bridgeCount.

- `Bridges.replacesTop(c)` means suppress the ordinary source-plane branch for
  that complete guarded dock cell. Native water/fringe tile planes also need
  suppression, because their original pixels contain projected timber.
- Existing `Bridges.append(cells,c,emitOpaque,uvFor)` emits the closed boards in
  absolute world coordinates after the normal elevation-lifting phase.
- `Bridges.water(c, NativeWater.surface)` returns `ts,mid,absoluteHeight` from
  a surrounding genuine water cell after water planning. Emit an ordinary water
  plane with this donor's live UV and call NativeWater.note for animation/body
  ownership. Do this after the elevation-lifting phase, without adding c.base.
- The water sheet stays below each board's underside. No guessed fallback
  surface is emitted if the planned ocean does not provide that clearance.
- c.bridge already suppresses shoreline soil, ordinary risers and bank-datum
  selection. Keep native water classification/permissions unchanged.

The provider owns atlas lifetime and animation; no separate image cache exists.
Missing/incomplete patterns or neighboring water donors retain native fallback.
Do not count a missing-window pattern as reviewed dock coverage. Scene-level
prefetching of complete small patterns can be added if native window QA shows
that fallback is visible.

## Verification

`tests/gen3_docks_test.lua` checks all three edition IDs, exact group ownership,
permission/layer/base immutability, rejection of altered or incomplete patterns,
no soil bank geometry, closed manifold edges for every board, source-footprint
bounds, two-pixel thickness, open rays above and below board gaps, and sampling
the final planned ocean datum. Existing Gen3Bridges tests retain Fortree and
Cerulean behavior. Production sources compile with LuaJIT 2.1.
