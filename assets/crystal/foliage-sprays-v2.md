# Original foliage sprays

Generated using the built-in image generation tool, generate mode. No reference
artwork was supplied to the tool. The PNG is the original RGBA output, copied
unchanged; it supplies the outer foliage cards, alongside the older inner crown
atlas. Quadrants: oak, pine, beech, shrub.

## Final prompt

Game production texture asset, original HD-2D foliage cutout atlas on a genuinely transparent RGBA background. One square image arranged as an exact 2 by 2 grid of four equally sized cells, with a generous empty transparent gutter between cells. Each cell contains ONE small branch spray, not a whole tree, not a ball or wreath, with 12 to 20 large individually readable leaves and some gaps of alpha between leaves. Top left: rich mid-green oak spray with broad lobed leaves, asymmetrical branching. Top right: blue-green pine spray, three tapered needle-covered twigs, needles grouped into readable flat clusters. Bottom left: fresh green beech spray with pointed oval leaves and gentle warm tips. Bottom right: dark emerald shrub spray with round waxy leaves. Every spray fits within its own quadrant without clipping. Orthographic front-facing foliage texture, original hand-painted stylized game art with clear geometric leaf silhouettes, restrained two or three tones per leaf, upper-left soft highlights, subtly darker lower leaves, no black outlines, no photorealistic micro-detail, no yellow speckle, no heavy cast shadow, no ground, no text, no labels, no background checkerboard. Designed to be mapped to overlapping foliage cards on 3D trees in a lush HD-2D pixel-character RPG. At thumbnail size the separate large leaves must remain readable. 1024 by 1024 composition.
